How to Use These Setups

1

Wait for Confirmation

Do not enter blindly. Look for price action confirmation at the entry zone (rejection, engulfing, break of structure, etc.).

2

Respect the Stop Loss

Place your stop exactly where indicated (or slightly beyond structure). Never move it further away.

3

Scale Out at TPs

Take partial profits at TP1, TP2, etc. Move stop to break-even after TP1 or TP2 hits. Let the rest run.

4

Manage Risk First

Risk only 0.5–2% of your account per trade. Position size = Risk amount ÷ distance to stop.

Non-Negotiable Risk Rules

  • ✓ Never risk more than 2% of your account on a single trade (1% is safer for beginners).
  • ✓ Always calculate position size before entering. Do not guess lot size.
  • ✓ If the setup is invalidated (price closes beyond the stop zone with strength), stay out.
  • ✓ Do not revenge trade. One good setup is better than five forced ones.
  • ✓ These are educational trade ideas based on price action — not financial advice.
