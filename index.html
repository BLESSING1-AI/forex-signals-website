FAQ

Straight answers

Is this investment advice?+

No. Apex Edge provides educational analysis and structured setups. You are solely responsible for every trade decision.

Can the AI change entry or stop levels?+

No. Levels are calculated by the deterministic engine. Gemini only returns APPROVE, WATCH, or REJECT with reasons and confidence.

What does TP1 → BE mean?+

Take profit 1 is the closest target. After it hits, stop-loss moves to breakeven (entry). Remaining size can run toward TP2 and TP3.

Why are there few signals on gold?+

Gold runs in strict London/NY session windows with higher quality filters and daily caps. Fewer, higher-quality setups by design.
