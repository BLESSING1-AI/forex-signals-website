How These Signals Are Generated

1. HTF Bias

D1 / H4 Structure

Only trade in the direction of clear Higher Highs / Higher Lows (bullish) or Lower Highs / Lower Lows (bearish). Conflicting HTF = no trade.

2. Zone

Area of Interest

Price must reach an unmitigated Order Block, S/R Flip, or Liquidity Sweep zone before any entry is considered.

3. Confirmation

M15 / M5 Trigger

Wait for Break of Structure or strong Rejection (Pin Bar / Engulfing) on the close of the candle. Never enter mid-bar.

TP1

1:1.5 RR or key M15 level. Close 30–50%. Move Stop Loss to Breakeven.

TP2

1:3 RR or H1 swing. Close another ~30%.

TP3

1:5+ RR or major H4 level. Close remaining or trail structure.
